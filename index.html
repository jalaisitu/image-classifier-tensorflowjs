    How it works

    Your image is processed in this browser. The app does not upload or save it on a server. An internet connection is needed to download the libraries and pretrained model.

    Google ViT predicts labels from its ImageNet categories. It can be wrong, cannot identify people by name, and does not learn from the images you select.
